# Platform Rules

Status: unverified

Platform rules were not verified during bootstrap. Before publication, verify
current official rules for LinkedIn and Product Hunt, target subreddit rules
for Reddit, and Hacker News launch norms.

Do not publish content, submit posts, automate comments, request votes, fake
community participation, or automate engagement from this repository.

## Platform Notes

- LinkedIn: final founder posts and company posts require owner-approved voice
  and claims.
- Product Hunt: listing copy, maker comments, FAQs, and gallery assets require
  owner approval before use.
- Reddit: each community needs separate rules review and disclosure handling.
- Hacker News: prepare technical notes and likely questions; do not fabricate
  founder story or traction.

# Trigger Behavior Review

Use these prompts to review description precision after edits.

## Should Trigger

- "Use this repo to prepare Product Hunt copy, gallery assets, and launch QA."
- "Create a LinkedIn PDF carousel with editable SVG sources for our product launch."
- "Inspect the product repository and build a claims map before launch."
- "Plan a Remotion demo video storyboard from the app routes and screenshots."
- "Initialize a marketing workspace for launch content and claim review."
- "QA these launch visuals for claim traceability, editability, and platform readiness."

## Should Not Trigger

- "Build a generic CRM with lead stages and sales pipeline reports."
- "Schedule posts to LinkedIn every morning."
- "Create a paid ads manager and campaign budget dashboard."
- "Redesign this unrelated settings page."
- "Edit this random wedding video unrelated to a product launch."
- "Make a generic logo mood board with no product launch or repository context."

The current description explicitly includes positive launch, positioning,
carousel, Product Hunt, Reddit, Hacker News, Remotion, visual, and QA triggers,
and explicitly excludes publishing automation, sales operations, unrelated UI
work, unrelated video editing, generic graphic design, CRM, and lead-management
implementation.

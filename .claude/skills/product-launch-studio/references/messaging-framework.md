# Messaging Framework

Build messaging from approved product truth and evidence. Separate strategic
messaging from channel drafts.

## Foundation Outputs

- target audiences and jobs to be done
- current alternatives and switching triggers
- category framing
- differentiators and proof hierarchy
- objections and answers
- CTA hierarchy
- message hierarchy
- voice guidance
- visual direction and font guidance
- campaign assumptions

## Constraints

Avoid broad audiences like "everyone" unless specifically justified. Avoid
fake urgency, vague hype, unsupported comparison claims, unsupported social
proof, and AI-writing patterns. Do not turn inferred facts into final copy
without approval.

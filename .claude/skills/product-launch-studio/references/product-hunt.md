# Product Hunt

Prepare launch materials, not publication automation.

## Outputs

- product name, tagline, short description, longer description
- maker comment draft and alternatives
- gallery image/video asset plan
- FAQ and likely objection answers
- launch-day checklist and review responsibilities
- claim traceability for every factual statement

Use only approved or campaign-permitted claims. Do not fabricate users,
reviews, rankings, awards, or maker experiences.

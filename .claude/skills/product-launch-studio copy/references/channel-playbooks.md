# Channel Playbooks

Use channel playbooks after product truth, claims, audience, campaign angle,
voice, and design direction are approved.

## LinkedIn

Founder posts may use first-person experience only when supplied or approved
by the owner. Company posts should be more direct and product-centered. PDF
carousels must have editable sources.

## Product Hunt

Prepare listing copy, gallery assets, maker comments, FAQs, and launch-day
review notes. Do not automate posting, comments, votes, or engagement.

## Reddit

Prepare community-specific research, rules review, transparent disclosure,
and non-spammy drafts. Do not fake participation or mass-post.

## Hacker News

Prepare founder notes, technical explanation, demo readiness, and expected
questions. Do not create publication-ready copy unless explicitly requested.

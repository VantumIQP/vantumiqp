# LinkedIn Carousel

Generate carousel manifests before rendering. Each slide should have a clear
job, claim references, accessibility text, and review status.

## Source Requirements

Every rendered carousel requires:

- one PPTX source
- one editable SVG per slide
- PDF derivative only after editable sources exist
- PNG previews as derivatives
- source screenshots kept separately and replaceable
- fonts-used and editability report

Do not flatten a PPTX into full-slide images. Do not render until the campaign
and claims are approved for rendering.

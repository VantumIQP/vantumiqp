# Hacker News

Prepare founder notes and launch readiness for a technical audience.

## Focus

- what was built and why
- concrete technical details
- honest limitations
- demo readiness
- expected skeptical questions
- claim traceability

Do not create publication-ready launch text unless explicitly requested. Do
not fake founder experience or community interaction.

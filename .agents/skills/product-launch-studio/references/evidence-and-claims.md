# Evidence And Claims

Every meaningful factual statement in final marketing content must trace to
one or more claim IDs in `marketing/claims.yaml`.

## Claim Statuses

Use: `verified`, `owner-approved`, `provisional`, `inferred`, `unverified`,
`conflicting`, `deprecated`, and `prohibited`.

Only `verified` and `owner-approved` claims may be used in final
publication-ready content unless the campaign explicitly permits provisional
claims.

## Evidence Map

Connect claims to source files, application routes, tests, screenshots,
recordings, docs, owner statements, or current official third-party URLs when
relevant. A claim is not verified merely because it appears in README text,
source comments, a landing page, an old deck, generated copy, or another draft.

## Claim Handling

When evidence does not support a statement, remove it, weaken it accurately,
or mark it as requiring approval. Preserve qualifications. Record prohibited
stronger variants so later drafts do not accidentally exaggerate.

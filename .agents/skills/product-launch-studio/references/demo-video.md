# Demo Video

Prefer real product interaction recordings over fabricated UI animation.

## Capture Workflow

1. Identify the user story being demonstrated.
2. Confirm the claims the demo proves.
3. Inspect real product routes and state.
4. Define seeded or synthetic demo data.
5. Create storyboard, shot list, and capture plan.
6. Capture deterministic interactions when safe.
7. Preserve raw recordings separately from edited derivatives.
8. Compose branded video in Remotion when implementation is requested.
9. QA facts, privacy, timing, captions, visuals, and render outputs.

Never capture credentials, API keys, tokens, private customer data, unrelated
tabs, notifications, bookmarks, passwords, or local-machine private details.
